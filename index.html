About

Hi! I am a postdoctoral fellow at KAIST AI, supported by the InnoCORE Postdoctoral Fellowship, where I work with Jaegul Choo at the DAVIAN Lab. I am also fulfilling my military service as Technical Research Personnel in the Republic of Korea Army. My research focuses on large language models, particularly their safety and their use as autonomous agents. Previously, I was a research intern at Krafton and Naver Webtoon. I received my Ph.D. from KAIST and my B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign.

I am always happy to chat about research, agents, or anything in between — feel free to reach out over email or coffee if you are around Seoul.
